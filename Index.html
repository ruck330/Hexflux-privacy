HEXFLUX Game

Privacy Policy | Last updated: November 1, 2026

HEXFLUX Game ("we", "our", or "us") provides HEXFLUX, a mobile puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

1. Game Progress

HEXFLUX stores gameplay information locally on your device, including scores, achievements, experience points, unlocked rewards, and settings. This information supports gameplay and customization.

2. Advertising

The planned monetized release of HEXFLUX uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Advertising personalization and consent options will be provided where required by applicable law.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-App Purchases

HEXFLUX plans to offer optional purchases, including Ultimate Edition and Remove Ads. Purchases will be processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment processing; HEXFLUX does not directly collect full payment-card details.

Purchase identifiers and entitlement information may be processed to validate purchases and restore purchased features.

4. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers supporting advertising and in-app purchases. We do not independently sell player information to data brokers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay data generally remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall HEXFLUX. Third-party providers may retain data under their own policies.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us using the email below.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the game. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

HEXFLUX will configure advertising and privacy protections according to its declared target audience and applicable children's privacy requirements. The game does not intentionally request personal information directly from children.

8. Your Choices

You may manage certain advertising preferences through Android or your Google account. Where legally required, additional consent or privacy controls will be available.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when the game, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will appear here with a revised date.
